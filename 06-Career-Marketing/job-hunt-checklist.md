# 📈 Career Marketing & Job Hunt Checklist

> **Objective:** The best DevOps engineer in the world won't get hired if nobody knows they exist. You must actively sell yourself starting in **November (Phase 4)**, *not* waiting until January when the plan finishes. 

---

## 📝 Phase 1: Resume Overhaul (Target: End of Oct)
*Don't wait until the curriculum is done to write your resume. You can add the skills as you acquire them.*

- [ ] Select a clean, standard single-column ATS-friendly template.
- [ ] Write a strong "Professional Summary" emphasizing Cloud, IaC, and CI/CD.
- [ ] **Experience List:** Tailor past IT/SysAdmin experience to highlight automation, scripting, and reliability.
- [ ] **Projects Section:** Prominently link the 5 GitHub Capstone repos (`04-Portfolio-Projects`).

## 🌐 Phase 2: LinkedIn Optimization (Target: Early Nov)
*LinkedIn is where the recruiters live. Your profile needs to operate as a net.*

- [ ] **Headline:** Change to `DevOps Engineer | AWS & Azure | Kubernetes | Terraform | CI/CD Builder`
- [ ] **About Section:** Write a compelling narrative about your passion for zero-trust infrastructure and automation.
- [ ] **Skills:** Add all 20+ DevOps skills you've mapped in the curriculum. Ask colleagues to endorse them.
- [ ] **Featured:** Pin your best 2 GitHub projects directly to the top of your profile.
- [ ] **Status:** Turn on "Open to Work".

## 🤝 Phase 3: Active Networking & Outreach (Target: Mid-Nov to Dec)
*Frictionless applications (Easy Apply) rarely work. You need to bypass the queue.*

- [ ] Connect with IT Recruiters specifically hiring for Cloud/DevOps.
- [ ] Send 5 targeted messages a week to hiring managers on LinkedIn.
- [ ] Join cloud tech Slack/Discord groups and answer questions to build authority.
- [ ] Track applied jobs in a structured spreadsheet (Company, Date Applied, Interview Stages).

## 🎙️ Phase 4: Interview Readiness (Target: December)
*Phase 5 video content will support this.*

- [ ] Practice 30-second "Tell me about yourself" elevator pitch.
- [ ] Prepare STAR method stories for: "Tell me about a time you broke production..."
- [ ] Prepare STAR method stories for: "How did you automate a painful manual process?"
- [ ] Run systemic mock-interviews with a recruiter or senior engineering friend.
